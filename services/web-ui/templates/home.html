What Helga does

Builds the course from real sources

Open textbooks and archives are swept before anything is written, and the course is checked against a published syllabus — you can see what it was built from.

Or from a book you upload

EPUB, PDF or text. The course follows the book's own structure — one lesson per chapter — and every concept is read from its chapter, not recalled.

Teaches by asking

Socratic dialogue with six question types and a difficulty that tracks you within a session, not a lecture with a quiz bolted on.

Checks you still have it

Spaced review schedules every concept, so a pass means you retained it months later — a stronger claim than a one-shot exam.

Whole degree programmes

Associate and bachelor's plans with real prerequisites. You choose the order, one course at a time, and each is built as you reach it.

Runs entirely on your machine

No cloud service, no account, no data leaving the device. The model and every course live here.
