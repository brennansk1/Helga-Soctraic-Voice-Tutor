    Before you sign — inspection checklist

    • Run the VIN through a history report (Carfax or AutoCheck) and check open recalls above.
    • Get an independent pre-purchase inspection (about $100–200). A seller who refuses is a walk-away.
    • Cold-start the engine; listen for ticking, knocking or rough idle, and watch for smoke.
    • Check all fluids: oil level and color, coolant, brake, transmission. Milky oil means walk away.
    • Look for mismatched paint, uneven panel gaps or overspray — signs of body repair.
    • Verify tire wear is even; uneven wear signals alignment or suspension problems.
    • Test every electronic: windows, locks, A/C both hot and cold, infotainment, cameras, all lights.
    • Drive at highway speed: check for vibration, pulling, transmission hesitation and brake shudder.
    • Confirm the title is in the seller's name and the VIN matches the dash and door jamb.
    • Get the out-the-door price in writing before discussing financing or a trade-in.
    • Decline dealer add-ons (VIN etching, nitrogen, paint protection) — they are nearly pure margin.
    • If financing at a dealer, arrive with a pre-approval from a bank or credit union to compare.
