## What was wrong last night, and is not now

- **Markdown stripping was eating SQL.** `SELECT *` and `price * 1.1` were
  reaching the learner as `SELECT` and `price 1.1` — in a turn explaining what
  `*` means. Code is now parked before emphasis is stripped.
- **Replies landed off-screen.** Answer a question and the response appeared
  below the fold with a "Latest" pill over your own words. Three causes: aids
  render after the text, content growth was unpinning auto-follow, and
  `scroll-behavior: smooth` meant every correction restarted an animation
  against a stale target.
- **Quiz and flashcards had never worked** — both proxies timed out at 30s for
  work that takes 13s and 162s.
- **The card lied twice**: it reported a stale build-time depth figure (39% for
  a course measuring 97.6%), and counted 95 provenance notes as things "worth
  reviewing".
- **The first lesson took two minutes** every morning. Now the model loads
  while you browse.

## Known and deliberate

- **Advanced SQL withholds one concept**, "NULL Value Impact": its Core
  Explanation is placeholder text and two repairs failed. The tutor refuses to
  teach it and the path shows it struck through. A gap, not a lie.
- **"Reading a Query Plan" is gated `needs_review`** — all four of its concepts
  are missing every section the tutor reads. "Fix and finish" resumes it.
- **Truth-checking is thin**, by measurement: MiniCheck runs, but the retained
  evidence is mostly source prefixes that do not reach the specific claims, so
  it reports *thin sourcing* rather than pretending to verify. That is the
  honest state, and the next real piece of work.
- **STT is down** — voice input is out of Mode A scope.
- **Security (P0.1) is still unaddressed and still yours to decide**: ports
  5002/5003/5006 bind `0.0.0.0`, `pipeline_api` can write course content with
  no auth, and `DELETE /api/courses` has none either.
