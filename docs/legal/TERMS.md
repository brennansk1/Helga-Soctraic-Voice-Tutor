# Helga Terms of Service — v1 (DRAFT, pending counsel review)

_Last updated: 2026-07-01. Policy version: `v1`._

1. **The service.** Helga is an AI Socratic tutor teaching the Utah Core
   Standards plus parent-approved electives. It supplements — it does not
   replace — a parent's or school's educational responsibility.
2. **Accounts.** One parent/guardian account owns one or more learner profiles.
   The parent is responsible for the accuracy of learner information and for
   all consents granted.
3. **Subscriptions & seats.** Paid plans set the number of active learner
   seats. Utah Fits All Scholarship expense documentation is available on
   request (see receipts in the billing portal once billing is live).
4. **Acceptable use.** No attempts to extract other families' data, probe the
   safety systems, or use the service to generate harmful content.
5. **AI-generated content.** Lessons and exam items are AI-generated against
   the published Utah standards and human-review gates. Errors are possible;
   report them and we will correct the catalog.
6. **Privacy.** The Privacy Policy (v1) is part of these terms.
7. **Termination.** Parents may delete learners or the whole account at any
   time; deletion is permanent for live data.

_Utah Fits All eligibility posture, mandatory-reporting posture, and liability
language require counsel sign-off before public launch (spec 08 §11)._
